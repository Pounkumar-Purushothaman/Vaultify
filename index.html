Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

Vaultify ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Vaultify is an offline-first secure vault. All card details (numbers, CVV, expiry dates) are encrypted and stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your personal financial data.

2. Data Security (AES-256)

Your information is protected using industry-standard AES-256 bit encryption. Your encryption keys are stored in the Android Keystore system, isolated from the rest of the OS.

3. Google Drive Sync

If you enable cloud sync, your data is stored in a hidden app-specific folder on your own Google Drive. Vaultify cannot see other files in your Drive, and other apps cannot see Vaultify's data.

4. Third-Party Services

We use AdMob for advertisements and Firebase Crashlytics for stability reporting. These services may collect anonymous device identifiers.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data instantly by removing cards within the app or clearing the application data in your Android settings.
